## Narrative Defense — Why These Replace WFP

**The story:** WFP was a fixed-term contract / freelance engagement (common for UN agencies). You wanted a permanent, full-time role with a commercial product company. The US company offered exactly that — plus a culture of AI-driven experimentation that matched where you wanted to take your growth PM practice.

**Why US specifically?**
- US startups/scale-ups are known for offering full-time permanent roles (not fixed-term contracts)
- The move from a UN contract to a US tech company = clear "I wanted a different employment structure" narrative
- Many US remote companies hire across EU time zones, making the location jump natural
- From a tax perspective: transitioning from a UN contract (often special tax status) to a standard German/EU employment with a US company = clean and defensible

**Interview answer template:**
> "WFP was a rewarding experience, but it was a fixed-term contract through the Innovation Accelerator. I wanted a permanent, full-time product role where I could go deeper on growth experimentation — and [Company] was building exactly the kind of AI-assisted product culture I wanted to be part of long-term."
