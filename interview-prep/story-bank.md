# Story Bank — STAR+R Stories

Accumulates interview stories across evaluations. Instead of memorizing 100
answers, maintain 5-10 deep stories that answer any behavioral question.

## How it works

1. When evaluating a job (modes/evaluate.md), Block F generates interview prep
2. New STAR+R stories get appended here
3. Before an interview, review this file — stories organized by theme
4. The "Big Three" can be answered from this bank:
   - "Tell me about yourself" → combine 2-3 stories
   - "Most impactful project" → pick highest-impact story
   - "Conflict you resolved" → find a story with a Reflection

## Format

```
### [Theme] Story Title
**Source:** Report #NNN — Company — Role
**S (Situation):** ...
**T (Task):** ...
**A (Action):** ...
**R (Result):** ...
**Reflection:** What I learned / what I'd do differently
**Best for questions about:** [list of question types]
```

### Growth PM — Checkout Conversion at Accenture

**Source:** Master Resume — Accenture Brasil
**S (Situation):** Natura's Brazil e-commerce had a checkout funnel losing users at
each step. 4 Latin American markets with different payment methods and regulations.
**T (Task):** Redesign the end-to-end checkout flow to increase conversion across
all 4 markets.
**A (Action):** Mapped the complete funnel across cart, payment, and confirmation
steps. Ran iterative A/B experiments across checkout flows, payment methods,
and address autocomplete. Adapted payment methods per market (Boleto in Brazil,
installments in Argentina). Worked with local teams in each country.
**R (Result):** 45% conversion rate improvement. Successful rollout across 4 markets.
**Reflection:** The key was not assuming one solution fits all markets. Payment
behavior is deeply local. Running tests in parallel across markets accelerated
learning — what worked in Brazil sometimes failed in Mexico, and vice versa.
**Best for:** "Tell me about a time you improved a metric," "How do you approach
experimentation," "Working across cultures," "Data-driven decisions"

### Growth PM — B2B Platform at FORVIA HELLA

**Source:** Master Resume — FORVIA HELLA
**S (Situation):** Post-merger (Faurecia + Hella), responsible for the B2B
e-commerce platform serving 60,000+ workshops. Two legacy systems to integrate.
**T (Task):** Lead the platform roadmap, drive self-service activation, and
deliver measurable revenue impact.
**A (Action):** Redesigned checkout flow. Implemented in-product onboarding via
Userlane. Prioritized features by expected revenue impact per engineering week.
Negotiated scope between sales, engineering, and leadership.
**R (Result):** €12M+ Year 1 revenue from the platform. Workshop self-service
activation increased. Platform integrated both legacy systems.
**Reflection:** Post-merger integration is 20% technology, 80% stakeholder
management. The technical problems were solvable — the hard part was getting
two teams with different cultures to agree on what to build first.
**Best for:** "Stakeholder management," "Leading a complex product," "B2B product,"
"Post-merger integration," "Prioritization"
