- The PM role split into **Builder PM** (ships prototypes, designs agentic workflows, evals) and **Integrator PM** (aligns engineering, data, commercial around AI products). Plain generalists are under the most pressure.

## The Primary Story: Postscript Infinity Testing

The Postscript work is an **AI-powered message optimization loop**, and it is the lead story for AI-heavy JDs:

1. **Perceive**: engagement data from SMS sends (CTR, conversion, earnings)
2. **Plan**: generative AI produces brand-aligned variants based on guidelines + past performance
3. **Act**: variants sent to real subscriber segments
4. **Observe**: predictive analytics scores performance against control
5. **Repeat**: model learns and improves the next batch

The human approval gate is the stop condition. This maps directly onto the evals vocabulary companies probe for: **evaluation criteria (brand fit, predicted CTR, earnings-per-message) gated what shipped.**

## Honest Framing Rules (binding)

- Postscript: "AI-powered message optimization loop" (or "engine") — accurate (generates, evaluates, learns, improves).
- GitHub/portfolio: "Builds production systems with frontier AI tools" — verifiable. NOT "expert in agentic AI".
- "Uses AI as a primary capability, not a side tool" — verifiable.
- "Ships prototypes with Cursor, Claude Code, and MCP" — verifiable from repos.
- Evals vocabulary is fair where it describes the Postscript work: "evaluation criteria", "scored against control", "quality bar", "model learning loop".

## What NOT to Claim

- "Loop engineering" — 2026 coding-agent discourse term, not applicable to Postscript.
- "Agentic systems designer" — unless the role specifically asks for it.
- "AI-native product builder" — unless the company uses this language.
- "Ships prototypes in afternoons" / "The spec IS the prototype" — meme language, not verifiable claims.
- Never invent model names, eval framework names, or metrics not in the master resume.
